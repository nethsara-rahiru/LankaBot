How it works

1. Local Rules First: The bot always checks your manual rules in rules.html first.

2. AI Fallback: If no rule matches, the message is sent to Groq Llama 3.1.

3. Custom Context: The AI uses your configurations along with the built-in AI scripts to generate a relevant response.

4. Custom Prompts: You can save prompt templates above to copy/paste and reuse as needed for your configurations.
