Human-like Interaction Tips

• Fastest responses: Set Typing Duration and Initial Delay to 0 for instant replies with no artificial wait.

• Human-like feel: Setting typing duration to 800–2000ms makes the interaction feel more natural and personal.

• Initial Delay: A 500–1000ms delay simulates reading time. Set to 0 to skip entirely.

• Typing indicator: Uncheck the typing indicator toggle above for the absolute fastest delivery — bypasses all timing delays.
