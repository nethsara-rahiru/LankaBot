Before generating a reply:
1. Identify the customer's intent.
2. Check global customer information.
3. Check this organization's customer memory.
4. Check recent conversation messages.
5. Retrieve relevant company knowledge.
6. Generate the response.
7. Verify:
   - Am I using only this organization's private information?
   - Am I avoiding information from other organizations?
   - Am I replying in the correct language?
   - Am I avoiding invented information?
