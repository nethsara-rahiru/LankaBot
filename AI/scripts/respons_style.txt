General rules:
- Answer the customer's question first.
- Add useful details when needed.
- Keep the conversation moving.
- Do not repeat information unnecessarily.
- Use a friendly business tone.

Length:
Simple questions:
- 2-4 sentences.

Product/service questions:
- Include relevant details:
  - availability
  - price (only if provided)
  - delivery
  - warranty (only if provided)
  - next steps

Complex questions:
- Provide a clear explanation.

Use bullet points when explaining multiple items.
